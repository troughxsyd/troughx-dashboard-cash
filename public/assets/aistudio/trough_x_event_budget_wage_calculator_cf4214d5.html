Commercial Framework

Standard Trough X Promoter Agreement ($5.00 Ticket Clip)

Standard venue hire structure executed with external promoters (e.g., BUSH, THROBB, POOF DOOF).

What Venue (Trough X) Captures & Retains

  • $5.00 flat clip per paid ticket sold (deducted from promoter settlement).
  • 100% of Beverage POS sales across all venue bars.
  • 100% of Cloakroom revenue ($5.00 per garment/bag).
  • $550.00 non-refundable booking deposit paid upon date confirmation.

What Venue (Trough X) Provides & Absorbs

  • Full operational staffing roster (Duty Manager, Floor Supervisor, Bar Staff).
  • Contracted crowd control security (1:100 ratio) & door scanner personnel.
  • Mandatory dedicated harm-minimisation medic & medical supplies.
  • Full post-event bio-wash and cleaning team.
